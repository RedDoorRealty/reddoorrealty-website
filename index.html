"You get what you pay for"

A higher fee doesn't buy more work. It pays for more overhead.

Some agents say a bigger commission means they'll work harder for you. But the extra percent or two doesn't add photos, showings or negotiating. It pays for franchise fees, office leases and team splits. Your home still goes on the same MLS, in front of the same buyers, and buyers pay what your home is worth, not what your agent charges.

Try this: ask any agent to name one thing they'll do for 5% or 6% that isn't on the list above. Then decide what that's worth to you.

Maine transfer tax

$2.20 per $500 of the sale price, split evenly between buyer and seller. On a $500,000 sale, your half is $1,100. A higher rate applies to the portion over $1 million.

Seller disclosures

Maine requires a property disclosure covering the water supply, septic, heating, hazards like radon and lead paint, and known defects. Randy walks you through every line.

Pre-listing checklist

  • Gather septic, well, heating and roof records
  • Test for radon, or have a recent result ready to share
  • Tidy the front door and walkway for photos

Questions sellers ask

Straight answers

How can you charge 1.5% and still be full service?

Red Door is an independent, owner-run brokerage with no franchise fees, no expensive lease and no team to split commissions with. Those costs are what push listing fees to 2.5% or 3% elsewhere. Your home gets the same marketing and the same hands-on work.

Doesn't a higher-paid agent work harder?

Not in a way that shows up in your sale. A bigger commission mostly covers the brokerage's costs: franchise fees, offices and splits with teams and managers. It doesn't buy extra marketing, extra buyers or a better negotiator. Judge any agent by what they'll actually do for you, in writing, and by their track record. Randy's full list of services is above, and he's glad to compare it line by line with anyone's.

Will my home sell for less with a lower-fee agent?

No. Your sale price comes from the market, the pricing strategy and how well the home is presented and negotiated. Randy does all of that. The fee only changes how much of the price you keep.

What does the 3.5% include?

Red Door's total commission is 3.5%: a 1.5% listing fee for Randy's full service, plus 2% offered to the agent who brings the buyer. Compare that with the 5% to 6% total most Maine sellers pay. Since the 2024 changes to commission rules, buyer-agent compensation is negotiable, and Randy will walk you through the right offer for your town and price.

Are there upfront fees?

No. You pay the listing fee only when your home sells and closes.

What if I'm unhappy with the service?

You can cancel the listing agreement without a penalty fee. Randy would rather earn your business than lock you in.

Can you help me buy my next home too?

Yes. Many clients sell and buy at the same time, and Randy coordinates both so the closings line up.

Which towns do you cover?

Most of Southern, Midcoast and Central Maine: Greater Portland, York County, the Lakes Region, the Midcoast and the Lewiston-Auburn area. See the full list of towns above, or call to ask about yours.
